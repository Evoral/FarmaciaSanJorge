# Proposal — Planilla de existencia inicial

> **Status:** approved (User decision 2026-11-08). Not implemented.

## Why

The existencia inicial works, but its way in is wrong for what it has to do:

1. It sits on top of `/partidas/ingresar`, the purchase form, one envase per submit. A pharmacy with hundreds of
   envases has no sheet, no import and no view of what it already counted.
2. A mistake is almost impossible to fix. A partida cannot be deleted; its lote and vencimiento cannot be edited; an
   `AJUSTE` only subtracts and needs the DT; a second partida of the same lote is refused by the unique index.
3. Two bottles of one lote cannot be loaded as two envases, although the spec says "every envase becomes a partida".
   The panel's "N envases" counter really counts partidas.
4. A bottle already expired on the shelf cannot be loaded (the purchase rule demands a future date). With the
   contralor digital, a controlled droga in that state would block the close forever.
5. The costo is mandatory per envase, although it no longer prices anything (`calcular-cotizacion.ts`, R27).

## What

Separate **counting** from **creating stock**.

- A tab **Existencia inicial** in `/partidas` holds a **planilla**: an editable draft, one row per droga + lote, saved
  as it is typed, that can be left half done and shared between people. It has no effect on stock.
- **Pasar a stock** turns the complete rows into stock, as many times as needed while the relevamiento is open. If
  the partida does not exist it creates it; if a partida de existencia inicial of that droga + lote + proveedor
  already exists, it adds to it.
- The pharmacy can work with recetas as soon as something is in stock, and keep counting.
- What already passed can be **corrected** while the relevamiento is open. Every quantity correction is a movement of
  its own, generated by the system.
- **Faltan cargar** lists the drogas with no existencia inicial yet: the second round.
- An **alert** warns before the plazo ends. When the DT closes or the plazo ends, everything becomes definitive.

## User decisions (2026-11-08)

- The tab is separate from "Nuevo ingreso"; the purchase page goes back to purchases only.
- The plazo rule stays as it is: it starts with the first signed cierre diario. It does not need to change for a
  pharmacy that loads before it starts signing.
- The stock is born when the user presses the button that passes the complete rows. There can be as many rounds as
  needed, and recetas can be loaded in between.
- Loaded stock can be edited until the DT closes or the plazo ends, with an alert days before.
- Quantity corrections are automatic and predefined: the user types the right figure and picks no motivo. They are not
  an `AJUSTE`.
- The DT co-signs only when a correction lowers a **controlled** droga.

## Decisions after review (2026-11-08)

A second agent reviewed the plan. Outcome:

- **Accepted:** Pasar a stock creates or adds, instead of refusing a lote already in stock. The "pass a whole droga at
  once" restriction is dropped: stock can be consolidated shelf by shelf.
- **Accepted in part:** the record of origin is never rewritten. That record is the `INGRESO_INICIAL` movement.
  `cantidad_inicial` cannot stay frozen, because `partida_cantidad_disponible_check` (disponible <= inicial) would
  refuse any upward correction and `/partidas` would read "480 / 100". It becomes the running total entered, moved
  only by the movement trigger. The double count the review feared cannot happen: the saldo is the stored
  `cantidad_disponible`, moved by delta, and never derives from `cantidad_inicial`.
- **Adjusted:** corrections are blocked when the relevamiento is not open, which covers the plazo as well as the DT's
  close. Pasar a stock works per droga + lote + proveedor group, not per row.

## Scope

Four deliveries, in order (`tasks.md`):

1. The tab, the panel out of "Nuevo ingreso", expired bottles allowed. No migration.
2. Planilla, Pasar a stock, Faltan cargar, the alert, the close's new checks. Migration 0124.
3. Correcting what already passed. Migration 0125.
4. Import from Excel into the planilla.

Delta spec: `specs/existencia-inicial/spec.md`.

## Out of scope

- Changing the plazo rule, the DT's close, the 90-day cap, the contralor saldo check (R6, R7's password, R8).
- Modeling "rounds" as a concept: a round is just another press of the button.
- Marking a droga as "not held": Faltan cargar is informative and blocks nothing.
- Changing the droga of a partida: it is taken to zero and loaded under the right droga.
- Editing lote or vencimiento of a partida already used: they may already be in the libro recetario.
- Undoing a Pasar a stock as a batch.
- Corrections after the relevamiento ends: those stay `AJUSTE DIFERENCIA_ARQUEO` with the DT (R11).
